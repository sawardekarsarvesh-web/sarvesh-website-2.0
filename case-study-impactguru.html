03 · Solution Offered

Saving lives and rewarding heroes

Original design scope

ImpactGuru Donor app gives all clarity and easy donation process to the user. Also, there are some features that app provides to the donor to choose particular cause, campaign, tax benefits, monthly donations, fund utilization reports etc.

  • Easier donation process
  • Cause & campaign selection
  • Tax-benefit information
  • Monthly donations
  • Fund utilization reports

Future roadmap (as stated in source)

In future roadmap to expand the feature there is one more benefits about rewards. Donor will get chance to win GEM rewards points. The GEM points will help user to claim the listed rewards.

Considering engagement usability factor the donor will get to play some gamification which include the daily task. Once donor completed the daily tasks he will received the rewards.

  • GEM rewards points
  • Rewards redemption
  • Gamification & daily tasks

07 · Define

Key Insights (with the help of interview)

  1. Insight 1

    To make a donor donate for the first time, we can pitch a campaign of a Child, urgent fund requirement, near to him/her and is about to close soon. (Show the remaining amount instead of percentage and goal amount).

    Design implication: Surface urgent, nearby, closing-soon campaigns (e.g. Urgent Fundraisers on Home) and show the remaining amount.

  2. Insight 2

    The thumbnail of the campaign can be a symptomatic photo and a second before-after image. The thumbnails can auto-rotate (like banners).

    Design implication: Use more informative, rotating campaign thumbnails.

  3. Insight 3

    The biggest hindrance is trust issue. If user gets regular updates:

    • when campaigner withdrawals funds
    • when campaign achieves goal amount
    • when patient gets discharge (a photo of patient would increase credibility)

    Design implication: Send regular campaign and beneficiary updates to build trust.

  4. Insight 4

    Instead of just words in update, a photos or voice recordings can be shared.

    Design implication: Allow photos and voice recordings in updates.

  5. Insight 5

    They need tax benefit and GEM plan updates properly.

    Design implication: Communicate tax benefits and GEM plan updates clearly.
